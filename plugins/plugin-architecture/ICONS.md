# Icons

The pictograms of `icon=` come from two collections. They were taken over unchanged from the predecessor project,
which generated them from these collections: Lucide icons in `src/icons/lucide.ts`, Simple Icons in
`src/icons/simple-icons.ts`. The statement tables list every name.

| Collection | Licence | Drawn |
|---|---|---|
| [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) | ISC, with portions from Feather under MIT | with lines |
| [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) | CC0 1.0 | filled |
